The First Front Page

How a school newsroom was born

FIRST NEWSPAPER IN SCHOOL HISTORY

Every school has a story, and every day at MGS something happens that deserves to be told: a match won in the last over, a debate that ran past the bell, a poem scribbled at the back of a classroom. For years those moments faded as quickly as they came. This November, that changes.

Message Chronicles is the first newspaper in the history of our school. Its idea was simple and a little bold: give students the pen, and let them report the daily matters of MGS themselves. Seven students were given that opportunity, and each arrived carrying something different.

Mahad Rehan, our Editor in Chief, came with the ideas and the vision. He saw a newsroom where others saw an empty table. Muhammad Bin Javed, the Associate Editor, brought judgment and order, shaping those ideas into a plan that could actually be printed.

Saad Malik arrived with his skills as a graphic designer, giving the paper its burgundy-and-gold face. Ayyan Sajid took charge of our social media, carrying the Chronicles from the newsroom to the screens of the whole school. Zaki Cheema, Abdul Hadi Maan and Ahmad Nawaz joined as journalists, ready to walk the corridors, ask the questions and write what they find.

Behind the team stands our patron, Miss Zainab Masood, whose guidance made all of this possible. Without her, this newsroom would still be an idea.

Chronicles carries a simple belief: the past, written for the present. What we report today becomes tomorrow’s history, and the school’s own students are the ones holding the pen. Welcome to Volume I, Issue I. This is where it begins.
